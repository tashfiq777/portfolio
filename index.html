02 / About Me

Turning mechanical intuition into verifiable engineering.

From Chattogram to RUET in Rajshahi — building a foundation in structural mechanics, fluid dynamics, and manufacturable design.

I'm Tashfiqur Rahman (Tashfiq), a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate (24 Series, Roll 2402077) at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET) and an active contributor to Team Crack Platoon, RUET's Formula Student engineering initiative.

What draws me to mechanical engineering is the honesty of physical laws: a chassis either resists torsion without excess mass, an airfoil either sustains attached flow or stalls, and a room either rejects heat or overheats its machinery. I work at the intersection of parametric 3D CAD in SOLIDWORKS and numerical simulation in ANSYS Mechanical and ANSYS Fluent to test ideas before metal is ever cut.

Whether benchmarking European Formula Student EVs for a 180° hairpin maneuver, refining a biased C-grid boundary layer around a NACA 0012 section, or modeling mated IC engine sub-assemblies, my goal is to grow into a high-impact CAE and mechanical design engineer capable of leading complex mobility, aerospace, and energy systems from concept to validation.

First-Principles Clarity
